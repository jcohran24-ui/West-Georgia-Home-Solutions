ADMIN CONTRACTOR MANAGEMENT UPDATE

WHAT "PENDING" MEANS
A new contractor application is saved with active=false.
Pending means you have received it but have not approved that contractor for active use.

WHAT APPROVE DOES
The new Approve button changes active=true.
The dashboard then displays Approved.
You can later Deactivate the contractor, which sets active=false again.

NEW CONTRACTOR ADMIN FEATURES
- Edit contractor application
- Approve contractor
- Deactivate approved contractor
- Delete contractor application
- Export contractor applications to CSV
- Edit service area, license, insurance, weekly lead limit, free leads remaining,
  exclusivity, plan type, notes, contact info, service and approval status.
